## Citation

If you use this repository, please cite the exact version that supported your
work. The machine-readable metadata is in [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff), and a
copyable BibTeX entry is in [`CITATION.bib`](CITATION.bib).

Preferred citation:

> REPLACE_AUTHOR_LIST (REPLACE_YEAR). *REPLACE_PROJECT_TITLE* (REPLACE_VERSION_OR_SNAPSHOT). OpenKaggle. REPLACE_DOI_OR_RELEASE_URL

- Version/tag: `REPLACE_VERSION_OR_SNAPSHOT`
- Source commit: [`REPLACE_COMMIT_SHA`](REPLACE_COMMIT_URL)
- Artifact manifest or checksums: [`REPLACE_MANIFEST_PATH`](REPLACE_MANIFEST_URL)

This repository does not replace the citations for official competition data,
upstream models, or third-party notebooks. Their sources, licenses, and
versions are listed in [`DATA_SOURCES.md`](DATA_SOURCES.md). User-authored
derivatives and large release assets are identified separately with their
provenance and checksums.
