# Security Policy

Do not open a public issue for credentials, private identifiers, restricted competition material, or a vulnerability that could expose another person or system.

Report sensitive findings privately to `jydu_seven@outlook.com`. Include the affected repository, a concise impact description, reproduction details, and any suggested mitigation. Please avoid accessing or copying more data than is necessary to demonstrate the issue. Response and remediation are provided on a best-effort basis; OpenKaggle does not promise an enterprise support SLA.

Historical research repositories may be archived and unsupported. Their README should state maintenance status and known runtime limits.
