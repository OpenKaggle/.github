# OpenKaggle Community Conduct

OpenKaggle is a shared workbench. Be curious, patient, specific, and kind.

- Welcome people with different experience levels, languages, backgrounds, and competition histories.
- Critique claims, methods, and evidence—not the person sharing them.
- Ask for clarification before assuming carelessness or bad intent.
- Make uncertainty, failed experiments, and corrections safe to publish.
- Do not use rank, medals, affiliation, or writing fluency to dismiss someone.
- Respect privacy, licenses, competition rules, and responsible disclosure boundaries.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, spam, or expose private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove material that breaks these expectations, and may limit participation when needed to keep the workbench safe. If a problem involves a maintainer, use the private contact listed in the organization profile rather than escalating publicly.
